# Notices and Attribution

## Originality

All content in this repository is an **original synthesis** written for this
skill. No third-party article, training material, or publication text is
reproduced here, in whole or in part.

Background reading during development included general professional-practice
writing on forensic accounting and internal auditing, some of it published by
commercial vendors as marketing content. None of it is quoted, paraphrased at
the sentence level, or reproduced. Where such material conflicted with a
primary source, the primary source was followed and the conflict is noted in
the relevant reference file.

## Primary sources quoted

**Federal Rule of Evidence 702** is quoted in
`references/damages-and-expert-report.md`. United States federal rules of
procedure and evidence are works of the federal government and are not subject
to copyright. The text was verified against the Legal Information Institute
(law.cornell.edu) on 19 September 2026; most recent amendment 2023.

## Standards referenced but not reproduced

**The Institute of Internal Auditors' Global Internal Audit Standards** are
referenced by name and described in general terms in
`references/audit-function-governance.md`. No standard text is reproduced, and
no standard number is quoted. The IIA owns that material, and the skill
instructs the reader to obtain the current text from the IIA directly rather
than relying on any secondary description, including this one.

The same applies to any auditing standards issued by the AICPA, the PCAOB, or
the IAASB, which are named where relevant and never reproduced.

## Product and vendor names

`references/tooling.md` names commercial software products for identification
only. All trademarks are the property of their respective owners. Naming a
product is not an endorsement, and the file opens with a standing warning that
product names, owners, and availability go stale and must be verified before
being relied on.

No pricing is stated anywhere in this repository, deliberately.

## Statistics and benchmarks

This repository contains **no statistics, salary figures, loss benchmarks, or
industry percentages**, by design. A number hardcoded into a reference file
rots silently and is then quoted as current. Where a benchmark is needed, the
skill directs the reader to obtain a current figure from the source and to
record the date it was checked.
